5 signs your business needs a custom web app

By GetBytes IT Solutions · 6 min read

Off-the-shelf tools are great until they are not. As your business grows, the workarounds add up — spreadsheets everywhere, copy-pasting between apps, and processes that only one person understands. Here are five signs it is time to invest in custom software.

1. You are paying for features you do not use

Generic platforms charge for a broad feature set. A custom app does exactly what you need — nothing more — and you own it.

2. Your team wastes hours on manual work

If staff spend time re-entering data or reconciling reports by hand, automation usually pays for itself quickly.

3. Your tools do not talk to each other

When your website, accounts and inventory live in separate silos, a custom system can connect them through integrations.

4. You cannot get the reports you need

Decision-making improves dramatically when the right numbers are one click away.

5. Growth feels harder than it should

If scaling means more manual effort rather than more output, software is the lever. Talk to us about where to start.
